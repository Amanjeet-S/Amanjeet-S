# Amanjeet Singh

## 1. About my work

I enjoy turning questions about financial risk and economic behaviour into mathematics that can be derived, implemented and tested. These projects grew from that curiosity and a desire to develop my abilities in probability, analysis, optimisation and econometrics. A recurring interest is how modelling assumptions shape what the evidence can support.

## 2. Projects

### 2.1. [Crash Insurance and the G10 Carry Premium](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Crash-Insurance-G10-Carry-Premium)

My interest in crash risk led to an examination of whether currency carry returns compensate for it. The analysis combines SABR volatility-smile calibration, protective-option pricing, portfolio backtests and dependence-robust inference, tracing how the insurance explanation changes across interest-rate regimes and how tail assumptions limit conclusions about skewness.

### 2.2. [Option Exercise-Policy Evaluation](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Option-Exercise-Policy-Evaluation)

The challenge of judging an option-exercise strategy motivated a closer look at policy loss, assessment error and computational allocation. Optimal stopping, regression Monte Carlo, conditional expectations and martingale bounds structure the analysis. In the model, tighter bounds improve assessment without changing the exercise policy.

### 2.3. [Deep Hedging: CVaR Replication](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Deep-Hedging-CVAR95-Replication)

I independently replicate a deep-hedging experiment to investigate when a learned hedge improves on delta hedging. Neural networks optimise conditional value at risk (CVaR) under GJR-GARCH dynamics, with Monte Carlo simulation and calibration checks. The estimated 95 per cent CVaR improvement remains uncertain; the difference strategy does not meet the paper's statistical-arbitrage criterion in this sample.

### 2.4. [Quantum Basket Pricing](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Quantum-Basket-Pricing) (private)

Curiosity about quantum computation led to this study of what accurate basket-derivative pricing would require. Gaussian conditioning, variance-reduction controls and interval error bounds connect the financial model to quantum resource accounting and classical numerical benchmarks. The project develops my understanding of the full pricing calculation; its synthetic comparisons do not establish practical quantum advantage.

### 2.5. [Monetary Policy](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Monetary-Policy) (private)

The possibility that postponed purchases are never fully recovered motivated this study of monetary tightening. Narrative policy shocks, regression-based cumulative responses and dependence-robust joint inference connect the economic question to the data. Durable-stock accounting distinguishes a rebound in monthly purchases from recovery of purchases missed earlier.

### 2.6. [Investment Memos](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Investment-Memos)

Writing equity research lets me work through why a business might be mispriced and what would overturn that view. Scenario valuation, financial-statement analysis and capital-structure modelling turn each thesis into explicit assumptions. The collection covers private-market valuations, LNG shipping and car rental.
