# Amanjeet Singh

## 1. About my work

I enjoy the point where a financial or economic question becomes a mathematical problem. I create these projects to explore risk, pricing and economic behaviour, and to develop my abilities in probability, analysis, optimisation and econometrics through derivation, implementation and evidence. I am particularly interested in how assumptions shape what a model can tell me.

## 2. Projects

### 2.1. [Crash Insurance and the G10 Carry Premium](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Crash-Insurance-G10-Carry-Premium)

I wanted to understand whether currency carry returns compensate for crash risk. I combine SABR volatility-smile calibration, protective-option pricing, portfolio backtests and dependence-robust inference. The study examines the insurance explanation across interest-rate regimes and shows how tail assumptions constrain what option prices reveal about skewness.

### 2.2. [Option Exercise-Policy Evaluation](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Option-Exercise-Policy-Evaluation)

I was interested in how reliably numerical methods can judge an option-exercise strategy. Optimal stopping, regression Monte Carlo, conditional expectations and martingale bounds let me distinguish policy loss from assessment error and study how to allocate computation. Tighter bounds improve the assessment while leaving the exercise policy unchanged.

### 2.3. [Deep Hedging: CVaR Replication](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Deep-Hedging-CVAR95-Replication)

I wanted to investigate when a learned hedge improves on delta hedging. I independently implement neural-network optimisation of conditional value at risk (CVaR) under GJR-GARCH dynamics, using Monte Carlo simulation and calibration checks. The estimated 95 per cent CVaR improvement remains uncertain; the difference strategy does not meet the replicated paper's statistical-arbitrage criterion.

### 2.4. [Quantum Basket Pricing](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Quantum-Basket-Pricing) (private)

Curiosity about quantum computation led me to ask what accurate basket-derivative pricing would require. I connect Gaussian conditioning, variance-reduction controls and interval error bounds with quantum resource accounting and classical numerical benchmarks. The project develops my understanding of the full pricing calculation; its synthetic comparisons do not establish practical quantum advantage.

### 2.5. [Monetary Policy](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Monetary-Policy) (private)

I wanted to understand whether households make up purchases postponed by monetary tightening. I combine narrative policy shocks, regression-based cumulative responses and dependence-robust joint inference. Durable-stock accounting motivates the central distinction between a rebound in monthly purchases and recovery of the purchases missed earlier.

### 2.6. [Investment Memos](https://github.com/Amanjeet-S/Investment-Memos)

I write equity research to work through why a business might be mispriced and what would overturn that view. Scenario valuation, financial-statement analysis and capital-structure modelling turn each thesis into explicit assumptions. The collection covers private-market valuations, LNG shipping and car rental.
